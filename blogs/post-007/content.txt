SQL Normalization Explained: 1NF, 2NF, 3NF (Five-Slide LinkedIn Carousel)

One phone number changes, and suddenly 47 database rows need updating. Sound familiar? 😅

That's why database normalization matters.

In this simple visual guide, let's understand 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF and how they help reduce redundant data and prevent inconsistencies.

Normalization is a great starting point for transactional databases, but analytical systems sometimes benefit from denormalization to simplify queries and improve reporting performance.

The trick is knowing when to normalize and when to denormalize, rather than applying the same approach everywhere.
