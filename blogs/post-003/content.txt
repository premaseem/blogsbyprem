Gamify a complex technology, and suddenly learning feels like solving a mystery. 🕵️

That’s exactly what happened when I tried The SQL Murder Mystery.

I started out practicing SQL. Soon, I was chasing clues, connecting evidence, and trying to figure out who did it.

Every query gave me a reason to keep going.

WHERE helped narrow the search. JOIN helped connect clues across tables. And an unexpected result meant it was time to rethink my assumptions.

What I liked most was how familiar that process felt.

A missing customer order. A payment that doesn’t add up. Duplicate records in a report.

Everyday engineering has plenty of mysteries of its own. We form a theory, query the data, and follow the evidence.

One useful reminder: a one-to-many JOIN can multiply your rows. Before celebrating a discovery, check whether your query created the surprise!

That’s why I like gamifying complex technology. Give someone an interesting problem, room to experiment, and a little curiosity, and learning becomes fun.
