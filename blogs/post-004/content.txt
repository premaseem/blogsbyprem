If you’ve been feeling stressed lately, just like me, there’s someone I’d love you to know.

AI is changing our work. Layoffs make the future uncertain. Deadlines, responsibilities, and the next promotion keep us on our toes.

Sometimes, the workday ends, but the mind stays logged in. Apparently, mine didn’t get the logout instructions. 🙂

This week in Austin, after years of hoping for the opportunity, I finally met Rajshree Patel, fondly known to me as Rajshree Didi. It felt like a lifetime wish coming true. 🙏

She’s the author of the bestselling book The Power of Vital Force. What makes her journey especially meaningful to me is where she started.

Before teaching meditation and breathwork, she was a prosecutor. She knew the demands of a profession where decisions carry serious consequences and people depend on your judgment.

Today, her work speaks to people like us: professionals navigating tight deadlines, big responsibilities, unclear expectations, and an environment that keeps changing.

We get so used to grinding that we sometimes forget we’re doing it. Even taking a deep breath starts to feel like something we’ll get to after the next meeting.

Her book brings ancient wisdom into everyday life through breathwork, meditation, and practical ways to understand a busy mind.

That feels relevant right now.

We spend so much energy updating our skills. The mind carrying all that pressure deserves some care, too.

A breathing practice won’t change a layoff decision or clear the calendar. But making space to pause can help us approach what comes next with a little more steadiness.

Meeting Rajshree Didi meant a lot to me. If you’ve been carrying more stress than you’ve been admitting, I’d encourage you to explore her work.
